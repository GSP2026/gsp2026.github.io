### Registration

Registration for GSP 2026 is now open. You can complete your registration [here](https://gac.afidcongresos.com/login_event.php?event=221). The registration fees are listed below.

Registration fees:
+ Student - 420 &euro; (Early Bird, until April 23) / 520 &euro; (Regular)
+ Non-Student - 520 &euro; (Early Bird, until April 23) / 620 &euro; (Regular)
+ Gold Attendee - 1.200 &euro; (Early Bird, until April 23) / 1.200 &euro; (Regular)

Early Bird fees are available until April 23, with regular fees applied from April 24.
